\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

 Treating the policy oracle as two typed set operations makes a missing element
a first-class violation. On one six-page fixture the production audit agrees with
the ground truth under oracle perception, the loop keeps its revisit edges, and
perturbed perceivers err as the set algebra implies. Three results qualify the design: a
vocabulary mismatch, fingerprint over-merges, and unsafe resumption. The evidence is fixture-level
(Sect.~\ref{sec:threats}); the browser-driven pipeline and the VLM channel remain
unevaluated, and next steps are in Sect.~\ref{sec:discussion}.
